# Notice to Microsoft, Xbox and Activision

This is an open letter from the people who maintain this repository to Microsoft Corporation, Xbox and Activision
Publishing, Inc., the owners of *Call of Duty: Black Ops* (2010).

## What this project is

- A fan project that runs *Call of Duty: Black Ops* (Zombies and Multiplayer) on hardware the game was never made
  for: Android handhelds and phones (arm64), Linux, and the web browser. It also lets the community change the engine
  itself in mods.
- Non-commercial. Nothing is sold, there are no ads, and we accept no donations for it.
- Not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Microsoft, Xbox or Activision. *Call of Duty* and *Black Ops* are
  trademarks of Activision Publishing, Inc.; all other trademarks belong to their owners.

## What we do not distribute

- None of the game's content. That means no fastfiles (`.ff`), IWD archives, videos, sounds, textures, models,
  scripts, menus or maps, in this repository, in the Android app or on any server we run.
- Every player has to copy the files from their own legitimately purchased PC copy of the game (Steam). The app does
  not download game data, and it does not work without the player's own files.

## How it was made

- The engine source in this repository is decompiled from the original PC game. The work that built on it was
  carried out with the help of an AI model: Anthropic's Claude, working through Claude Code under the direction of
  this project's maintainers. That work covers the ports to Android, Linux and the browser, the Vulkan renderer
  bridge, touch and controller input, offline menus, co-op and fixes to decompilation errors.
- This is an experiment in what AI-assisted development can do with an older game, and in new ways to play an older
  title.
- It is meant in no way to infringe on your work, compete with your products, or take anything away from them. It
  is only useful to people who already own the game, and we hope it brings some of them back to buying it.

## Why now

On October 13, 2026 Xbox announced [XBOX LABS](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/10/13/introducing-xbox-labs/):
"a small group of game makers, engineers and scientists whose job is to turn emerging ideas into things our players
and developers can experience". The announcement spoke of "giving old games new life", of building "in the open",
and of AI agents as a new way to make software.

This project took its inspiration from that announcement. It is a small, community-made example of the same ideas
applied to a game we love. It is not part of XBOX LABS and makes no claim of approval by it.

## If you want something changed or removed

We will respect any request from Microsoft, Xbox or Activision. If you want something changed, or the project taken
down, open an issue on this repository or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile
([Zephelinn](https://github.com/Zephelinn)). We will act on it promptly and without dispute.

Thank you for *Black Ops*.
